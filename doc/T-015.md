Status: locally verified functional priority for review; design

Branch: `feature/SOL-05-resource-recovery`, from merged PR #15 (`07349c8`),
with the existing planning delta retained. It supersedes DRY-first sequencing and
further heuristic or cosmetic increments. Full T-014/T-015/T-021 and the remaining milestone gates stay
open. The fixed caps and exclusions below remain historical T015-P1 conditions;
they do not prohibit this explicitly requested resource-policy work.

Governing requirements: SOL-05/SOL-08, affected SOL-04 and AT-10/AT-16/AT-17;
spec section 6.6, authoritative validation, and the existing count-only
publication/Save/export contract. Freeze timed-versus-fixed-work semantics and
resolved-budget/thread reporting in a decision record with compatibility impact
before changing behavior. Do not silently reinterpret existing saved runs.
ADR 0019 specifies the cap/reserve propagation, finite recovery ladder,
completed-stage reuse, versioned timed page order, exact fixed-work replay,
additive contract fields and practical red/green implementation sequence.

- **Deliverable:** continued useful CPU search under recoverable resource pressure,
  with a hardware-aware host budget, bounded recovery and honest termination.
  Hitting one operation's allowance must not automatically terminate the job.
- **Simplest approach:** extend the existing serial solver/controller and resource
  admission paths. Resolve the initial host ceiling from 50% of available RAM,
  honoring a lower user cap and complete live-owner/scratch accounting, including
  retained preparation, validation and publication. Propagate the resolved
  operation-wide ceiling through nested limits; replacing only the top-level
  512 MiB constant is insufficient. Recheck
  pressure at safe boundaries; evict disposable data, reduce retained batches or
  worker concurrency when that lowers the required memory, then retry boundedly.
  Preserve manual pitch and physical settings. Keep the last validated incumbent
  while an unfinished trial is discarded or resumed. Geometry/compute report
  outcomes; retry policy stays in the coordinator. Recreate failed/interrupted
  FFT execution owners when their lifetime contract prohibits reuse.
- **Work policy:** separate reproducible explicit fixed-work runs from timed
  interactive search. Timed operation allowances may yield or receive further
  bounded work while the total Start deadline remains; do not blindly reset a
  failing counter and recompute the same work. Cumulative work counters never
  reset; prefer completed-stage reuse and retry only after a meaningful budget or
  residency change. A local failure may defer that path and allow other
  implemented affordable work, including usable completed candidate pages without
  waiting for every orientation. Record any ordering change for reproducibility.
  Single-thread execution does
  not reduce total work or eliminate shared grid storage. No idle retry loop may
  be presented as continued search. Stop, deadline, explicit fixed-work completion,
  natural search exhaustion, unrecoverable failure or no remaining affordable
  work can terminate while retaining the valid result. Clarify in the decision
  record that manual-pitch terminal preflight rejection follows exhausted recovery;
  a recoverable local denial is not permission to change pitch.
- **First observable result:** a practical analytic reproducer forces resource
  pressure, observes a real recovery transition in the same job, and subsequently
  produces another independently native-valid candidate/count improvement.
- **Expected scope:** existing solver, geometry/FFT operation boundaries,
  CLI/Rust resource resolution and minimal UI/contract reporting, plus focused
  tests. One implementation owner and one appropriate reviewer; no new scheduler
  subsystem. Reassess at roughly 1,000 added handwritten production lines or any
  unplanned execution path. Do not use the checkpoint to reduce the user outcome
  to a larger constant or a more descriptive error message.
- **Exclusions:** GPU, new rotation modes, new strategy portfolios, repair,
  geometric simplification, physical constraint changes, silent manual-pitch
  changes, a general job framework, and implementation of the deferred DRY pass.

Acceptance must include:

2. Resolved RAM/user ceiling and actual thread changes are recorded and visible.

3. Stop/deadline remain responsive through recovery; invalid or indeterminate

4. On this PC, full accepted Pryanik-2 at manual 2 mm, 400 x 340 x 285 mm,
