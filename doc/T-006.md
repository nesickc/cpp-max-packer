## Observable acceptance

- Find and independently validate all 64 copies of a 10 mm cube in a fixed
  40×40×40 mm box with zero gaps. Physical candidates do not depend on voxel pitch.
- Retain 64 copies in the 45 mm clearance fixture, with independent checks of
  1 mm pair and wall distances. Keep separate clearances and physical dimensions.
- Produce an explicit validated empty `best_found` when the bounded baseline
  finds no placement; report termination without an impossibility claim.
- Enforce the context's rotation permissions, deterministic candidate ordering
  and physical grid placement. Exercise representable boundary cases and
  conservative arithmetic around grid counts and translations.
- Use accepted STL geometry for containment. Analytic concavities and excluded
  cavities must reject enclosing-box candidates that cross forbidden material.
- Retain immutable validated incumbents. Rejected/indeterminate candidates,
  exhausted work and failed allocations cannot replace a valid best with a worse
  or unvalidated layout. Count precedes occupied Z extent and deterministic ties.
- Compute utilization from accepted object material and fixed container volume;
  omit unavailable volume evidence. Do not use a voxel or cluster-box estimate.
- Bound candidate work and retained/scratch storage. Keep observable counters,
  termination and snapshots for deterministic replay and fault checks.
- Certify requested wall thresholds for arbitrary cardinal solids and ambiguous
  generic rotations inside analytic or certified STL boxes. Use bounded exact
  coordinate proofs; retain true nonconvex STL containment and reject unresolved
  placements. [ADR 0008](../spec/decisions/0008-physical-baseline.md) records the
  compatibility change and validation kernel revision.
