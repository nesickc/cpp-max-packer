Status: planned; no implementation or passing evidence claimed.
Proposed branch: `feature/SOL-03-orientation-refinement` (not created).

Start the branch only after that prerequisite and this planning change merge.

## Scope and boundary

Add Upright and Free 3D engine catalogs and desktop controls, with bounded
translation and permitted continuous angular refinement. Preserve Fixed, Cube
and Custom restrictions. Record the resolved catalog, ordering/version and hash.
Multiple starts and neighborhood reinsertion are T-015; automatic presets are
T-016. Sampling does not establish exhaustive coverage or an optimal packing.

## Governing requirements

SOL-03/SOL-04/SOL-07, GEO-02/GEO-06 and UI-01/UI-03; AT-08, affected
AT-06/AT-09/AT-12 and AT-17 subsets. Canonical spec §§5.2–5.3, 6.4–6.6,
7.1 and 8.3 govern. This contributes to M4 without claiming its complete gate.
[ADR 0011](../spec/decisions/0011-cpu-first-follow-up.md) and shared evidence apply.

## Observable acceptance

1. **T014-A1:** Upright initially samples yaw every 15 degrees and permits only
   yaw refinement; source +Z remains world +Z. Fixed, 24 Cube and discrete Custom
   policies never gain forbidden rotations during refinement or replay.
2. **T014-A2:** Generate Free catalogs in the §6.4 order: sorted proper Cube
   rotations, `R_cube * R_z45`, then Halton bases 2/3/5 from index 1 with the
   specified XYZW map. Deduplicate antipodes; reproduce hashes for 256/2,048 entries.
3. **T014-A3:** On `tilted_bar`, Cube returns a validated zero-copy packing;
   the explicit 45-degree Z catalog and Free each find at least one. Use its
   canonical dimensions, box and gaps, with independently checked transforms.
4. **T014-A4:** Reject non-finite and zero Custom quaternions; normalize valid
   inputs and deduplicate q/-q. Save/reload preserves canonical order, version,
   permissions and resolved hash; unsupported versions fail clearly.
5. **T014-A5:** Exercise translation steps h/2, h/4, h/8 and Free angular steps
   5/2/1 degrees. Independently validate every accepted move and rebuild/update
   affected fields; rejected or indeterminate moves cannot replace the incumbent.
6. **T014-A6:** Controls show mode, supported catalog size and fixed/custom
   inputs. Restriction edits start a new job; stale results keep their original
   settings. Record early refinement termination and actual candidate/pass work.
7. **T014-A7:** Repeat fixed-work CPU runs at the same build/thread configuration
   with identical counts and transforms. Bound preprocessing/catalog caches and
   worker memory; cancellation leaves the last complete validated result usable.

## Tests, evidence and review

Observe spec-linked failing catalog/refinement cases before implementation.
Use independent signed-permutation/rotation checks, tilted-bar geometry and
adversarial permission tests rather than only regenerating the implementation.
Retain seeds, catalogs/hashes, counts/poses and complete logs in `.local/t014/`.
Routine independent code/contract review applies; numerical orientation admission
or shared-state concurrency changes require focused critical review.

## Open decisions and compatibility

Freeze catalog versioning, permitted-refinement representation, deterministic
ordering, scheduling and desktop/schema compatibility before parallel edits.
Do not silently reinterpret existing Fixed/Cube/Custom saved settings. Exact
continuation compatibility belongs to T-017/T-018; a regenerated catalog must
not masquerade as the old catalog. No new dependency/version is selected here.
